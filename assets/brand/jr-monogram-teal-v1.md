Created with the built-in image generation tool from `jack-rocca-logo-teal.original.png`.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished JR monogram icon derived directly from the supplied Jack Rocca signature reference. The reference is transparent; ignore the apparent black backdrop and teal haze in its preview.
Text: "JR" only.
Combine just the capital J and capital R from this signature into one compact, connected mark. Preserve the original letterforms as closely as possible: right-slanted calligraphic capital J with its curled top terminal and sweeping lower-left loop; capital R with its broad rounded upper bowl and elegant sweeping lower-right leg. Bring the two capitals together so their central strokes and the J's returning swash connect naturally, retaining clear readable J and R silhouettes. Remove all lowercase letters. Do not substitute a generic script typeface or invent a geometric monogram.
Solid deep teal #0b7474 ink, clean smooth edges, variable-width pen strokes faithful to the reference. No glow, shadows, gradients, texture, frame, badge, border, extra symbols, or other text. Transparent background with genuine alpha, not a checkerboard drawing. Square 1024x1024 composition with the monogram centered, balanced and generously sized, about 80% of the canvas, all flourishes fully visible and modest clear space on every side. A crisp restrained personal website icon; one mark only, no presentation board or mockup.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit this JR monogram. Preserve the exact teal JR letterforms, their connected central stroke, placement and proportions. Replace the entire gray checkerboard and all background texture with perfectly uniform pure white #FFFFFF, including inside the letter loops. The letters should be uniformly solid deep teal #0b7474 with clean smooth antialiased edges, no texture, grain, lighting, shadows, gradients or glow. Plain flat two-color logo artwork, not a mockup. Keep the whole mark visible. Square canvas. Do not draw checkerboards. Do not change the shape or lettering.
